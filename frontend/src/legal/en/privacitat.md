## 2. What data we process and why

### If you create an account

- **Data:** your name, email address and password, of which only an irreversible digest (hash) is stored. We also store the date you signed up and the date and version of the terms of use you accepted. [[PENDENT: the date and version of the accepted terms are not stored yet.]]
- **If you sign in with Google:** Google sends us your Google identifier, your email address and your profile name, and we store them. Google also sends us your profile photo, which we do not store. If you already had an account with the same address, it is linked to it and, from then on, you sign in with Google.
- **Purpose:** to manage your account and identify you when you sign in.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the terms of use (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).
- **Is this data required?** Yes: without it, the account cannot be created.

### If we have invited you to create an account

While registration is by invitation only, we store the email addresses of invited people (and, if needed, a short note to know who they are) so that they can create an account. You or the person who suggested you gave us this address. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in opening the service gradually (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### If you write to us

We process your address and your message in order to reply. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, if you exercise any of your rights, compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### Site security

As with any website, the server receives your IP address. We use it only in memory, for one minute, to limit the number of requests from each address and protect the site from abuse; we do not store it. If a server error occurs, a technical log is kept so that it can be fixed. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and running (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Profiling and automated decisions

We do not profile you or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 3. Who we share data with

- **Hosting:** OVH SAS, in France (European Union), as processor.
- **Email:** the messages you send us are stored by Proton AG, in Switzerland, as processor. Switzerland has an adequacy decision from the European Commission.
- **Google:** if you press “Continue with Google” in the sign-in window, your browser loads Google's identity service from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and technical data about your browser and may use their own cookies (see the [Cookie policy](/legal/cookies)). If you sign in, Google sends us the data in section 2. Google Ireland Limited processes this data as controller, under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Map:** map images are downloaded from the servers of the Institut Cartogràfic i Geològic de Catalunya (ICGC), which receive your IP address.

Apart from these cases, we will only disclose your data where required by law.

## 4. How long we keep it

- **Account:** for as long as you have it. If you ask us to delete it, we will do so within one month at most.
- **Backups:** None are made at the moment.
- **Invitations:** until you create your account or we withdraw the invitation. [[PENDENT: not yet deleted automatically when the account is created.]]
- **Messages:** for as long as needed to reply and, if you exercise a right, for as long as needed to show that we dealt with it.
- **IP address:** one minute, in memory only.
- **Error logs:** overwritten automatically when they reach a maximum size.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, object to or restrict its processing, or receive it in a portable format. Write to [{{titular.email}}](mailto:{{titular.email}}) (if you have an account, from its address). We will reply within one month at most. You can also lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)](https://www.aepd.es).

## 6. Minimum age

You must be 14 or older to create an account. If we find an account belonging to someone under 14, we will delete it.

## 7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its update date. If the change is significant, we will let you know before it applies.
