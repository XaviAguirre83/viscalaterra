# Cookie policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser; local storage (localStorage) is a similar mechanism. These are the ones viscalaterra.cat uses.

## 1. Our own cookies and storage

- **`__Host-sessio`** (cookie): keeps you signed in to your account. It is created when you sign in and expires after 7 days or when you sign out. It is technical and necessary for the service you request, so it does not require your consent.
- **`viscalaterra-idioma`** (local storage): remembers the language you chose. It is only stored if you change the language, and it is kept until you delete it. It is a preference you choose, so it does not require your consent.

## 2. Third-party cookies

If you open the sign-in window, your browser loads Google's identity service to show the “Continue with Google” button. This service stores the **`g_state`** cookie on our domain, which, according to Google, remembers whether you have signed out so that you are not signed in again automatically. [[PENDENT: duration, check it in the browser.]] Google may also use its own cookies, under its [cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies). If you do not open the sign-in window, nothing from Google is loaded. [[PENDENT: confirm that these cookies do not require consent (option a of decision L10).]]

## 3. What we do not use

viscalaterra.cat does not use analytics, advertising or social media cookies. That is why it does not show you any banner asking for your consent.

## 4. How to delete or block them

You can do it from your browser settings. If you block cookies, you will not be able to stay signed in. [[PENDENT: check what happens with the Google button.]] If you block local storage, the site will not remember your language.
